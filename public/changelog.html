Split-screen redesign, contact rework & dark-mode polish

Unreleased
  • ChangedHome — the opening screen now uses a split layout: the introduction, buttons and tech stack sit on the left, and the photo moved onto a highlight card on the right with at-a-glance badges (“6+ years”, “Gen AI · RAG · MCP”, “Legal-tech · Fintech · Real Estate”). Same content as before, quicker to scan — in both light and dark theme.
  • ChangedEvery section now follows the same split design: the section title sits in a rail on the left with an at-a-glance summary — About's stats, the project and recognition counts, core competencies, education highlights — while the detailed content scrolls on the right. On phones everything stacks as before.
  • ChangedExperience reads like a CV spread: every role, date range and promotion sits in the left rail as a compact career timeline, with each company's highlights in cards on the right.
  • ChangedContact — the LinkedIn, email and GitHub cards moved next to the section title on the left, so the message form gets the full main column.
  • AddedSkills — a new “Coding Assistance” category (Claude, Cursor, Codex), a CI/CD badge on the DevOps card, and the Core Competencies badges now spotlight the daily stack — Java, Spring Boot, GoLang, MongoDB, LLM, Prompts and Scalable Systems — replacing Cloud Computing and Leadership.
  • ChangedContact — the “Ready to Collaborate?” invitation and the message form are now a single card instead of two, and it leads the section, with LinkedIn / email / GitHub cards underneath.
  • RemovedThe separate “Start a Conversation” button; LinkedIn is still one click away from its own card.
  • FixedHobbies cards were nearly invisible in dark mode — the tinted surfaces matched the section behind them. Cards are now a step brighter with a defined edge, and their text moved up to stay legible.
  • FixedExperience, Projects and Achievements sections had the same washed-out backdrop in dark mode; their gradient is now darker so cards read as raised.
  • AddedThis changelog page at /changelog.html, theme-aware and linked back to the portfolio.
  • ChangedFooter copyright now reads © 2018 - Present, Mohd Shoaib Rayeen, covering the site from its first commit.

Light & dark theme

26 Jul 2026
  • AddedVisitor-selectable light and dark theme with a sun/moon toggle in the header, on desktop and mobile.
  • AddedTheme choice persists across visits in localStorage, and applies before first paint so a reload in dark mode never flashes white.
  • AddedDark styling across every section, tuned so all text clears WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
  • ChangedLight theme is the default and is visually unchanged for anyone who does not switch.
  • AddedAccessible names on the theme toggle and the mobile menu button, plus tests for switching, persistence and keyboard operation.
